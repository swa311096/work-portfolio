# Google vs Meta vs TikTok

The cleanest way to compare these three platforms is:

- **Google captures demand**
- **Meta predicts demand**
- **TikTok creates demand through creative**

That one distinction explains most of the structural differences between them.

## 1. Where Intent Comes From

| Platform | Intent source | What that means |
|---|---|---|
| Google | Explicit query intent | The user tells the platform what they want |
| Meta | Inferred identity and behavioral intent | The platform predicts likely interest before the user asks for anything directly |
| TikTok | Entertainment and content-affinity feedback | The platform uses content engagement to discover what may become commercially relevant |

Google starts with declared demand.  
Meta starts with prediction.  
TikTok starts with engagement and tries to turn that into commercial opportunity.

## 2. What Advertisers Use Each Platform For

| Platform | Strongest advertiser job |
|---|---|
| Google | Capture existing demand efficiently |
| Meta | Find and scale likely responders across broad audiences |
| TikTok | Generate demand through native-feeling creative |

That is why:

- Google is strongest when the user already knows enough to search
- Meta is strongest when the advertiser wants scale through predictive delivery
- TikTok is strongest when the product can be sold through creative-led discovery

## 3. Where The Value Comes From

| Platform | Main value source |
|---|---|
| Google | Access to explicit commercial intent |
| Meta | Large-scale prediction using identity, behavior, and conversion signals |
| TikTok | High-engagement discovery where creative can create demand |

In simple terms:

- Google reduces guesswork
- Meta reduces manual audience selection
- TikTok reduces the distance between entertainment and conversion

## 4. What Matters Most: Targeting Or Creative?

| Platform | Targeting importance | Creative importance |
|---|---|---|
| Google | High, but query is the main anchor | Important, but usually less central than intent |
| Meta | Very high because prediction quality drives delivery | Still important, especially with broader targeting |
| TikTok | Important, but less decisive than platform-fit creative | Extremely high because creative itself helps create demand |

The rough rule:

- Google wins on intent
- Meta wins on signal density
- TikTok wins on creative resonance

## 5. Why Measurement Feels Different

| Platform | Measurement feel | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google | Cleanest | Search-to-click-to-conversion path is often direct |
| Meta | Medium trust, more modeled | More inference, more privacy pressure, more dependence on CAPI and modeled attribution |
| TikTok | Often noisier | Discovery-led journeys are less deterministic and more creative-dependent |

This does not mean Google is perfect or TikTok is weak. It means the underlying user journey creates different levels of measurement clarity.

## 6. Main Strategic Tradeoff

| Platform | Main tradeoff |
|---|---|
| Google | Expand monetization without hurting query trust |
| Meta | Automate aggressively without making the system too opaque |
| TikTok | Lower the creative barrier without reducing native quality |

These are not side issues. They are the core product problems for each ads business.

## 7. When I Would Reach For Each Platform

- Use **Google** when demand already exists and capturing it efficiently is the priority.
- Use **Meta** when the goal is scaled demand generation plus performance marketing driven by strong signals and broad delivery.
- Use **TikTok** when the product can win through native creative, discovery, and creator-style content.

## 8. The Simplest Mental Model

If I had to compress the difference into one line each:

- **Google:** the user already asked
- **Meta:** the platform predicts who may care
- **TikTok:** the creative makes the user care
