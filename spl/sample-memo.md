# Sample Memo

Mercor proposes a 12-week pilot to deliver high-quality expert data that improves agentic AI performance across Microsoft's enterprise support workflows. We recommend starting with a Support Resolution Evaluation Set: a structured benchmark that measures how well AI agents handle complex, multi-turn support scenarios before Microsoft expands into larger post-training data programs.

As part of this pilot, Mercor will deliver 2,000 accepted prompt-rubric evaluation records across Microsoft enterprise support workflows. Each record will include a realistic support prompt, relevant workflow context, and an expert-written rubric for evaluating model response quality. Initial workflow coverage will include Microsoft 365 admin and configuration support, Azure setup and troubleshooting, Teams/Copilot enterprise support, billing and subscription support, and escalation or policy-boundary cases.

We recommend this domain because enterprise support is highly relevant to Microsoft's AI roadmap, operationally measurable, and well suited for agentic systems that need to reason through user intent, product constraints, troubleshooting steps, and escalation boundaries. After validating the evaluation layer, future phases can expand into ideal responses, pairwise preference data, and multi-turn tool-use trajectories for end-to-end agent training.

Mercor will source and vet an 80-100 person expert bench across creator, reviewer, and senior reviewer roles. Experts will be selected for experience in Microsoft administration, Azure support, enterprise IT support, technical troubleshooting, support QA, and escalation management. The attached project plan outlines the expert sourcing funnel, ramp schedule, task allocation, and review structure.

To ensure quality, Mercor will run a calibrated production process across three stages: task creation, expert review, and rework/sign-off. Each record will become delivery-ready only after expert review confirms accuracy, rubric quality, workflow relevance, and formatting completeness. Mercor can operate in Microsoft's preferred annotation environment or in Mercor's controlled annotation workflow, depending on Microsoft's security and integration requirements.

Mercor will price the pilot using a tiered expert-hour model across creator, reviewer, and senior reviewer capacity, with a Mercor platform and project management fee applied on top of expert costs. The attached project plan includes the detailed cost model, target margin, weekly ramp, quality controls, and delivery milestones.

As next steps, we would ask Microsoft to confirm the proposed workflow scope, preferred annotation environment, and review cadence for the initial calibration batch. Upon approval, Mercor can begin expert sourcing and project setup immediately, with the first calibrated sample batch delivered within two weeks.
